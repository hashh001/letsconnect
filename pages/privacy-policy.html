Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

ECA-Connect is built with privacy by design. We collect only what is necessary to match you with groups and activities near you, and we never sell your data.

1. What We Collect

We collect the following information when you use ECA-Connect:

  • Account information: Your display name and profile photo (if provided). Your email address is stored only in Firebase Authentication and is never written to our database.
  • Location: An approximate location (rounded to ~1–2 km accuracy). We never store your precise GPS coordinates. Only the city/area name and a fuzzy coordinate pair are saved.
  • Availability: The days and time windows you mark yourself as free.
  • Interests and preferences: Activities you enjoy, skill levels, language preferences, and search radius.
  • Group activity: Groups you create, join, or request to join.
  • Optional: A bio and social media links you choose to add to your profile.

2. What We Do Not Collect

  • Your precise GPS coordinates (only an approximate area is stored)
  • Your email address in our database (stored only in Firebase Authentication)
  • Payment or financial information (we have no paid features)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Browsing history or cross-site tracking data

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Match you with groups and activities near your approximate location
  • Display your profile to members of groups you have joined
  • Enable group creators to review join requests
  • Send you notifications about group activity (within the app)

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the matching service.

4. Who Can See Your Data

  • Your full profile (including location, availability, preferences) is visible only to you.
  • Your public profile (display name, photo, bio, interests, group stats) is visible to other authenticated users when you share your profile link or when you are a member of a shared group.
  • Your availability and gender preference are visible to members of groups you have joined.
  • Your approximate location (city name only) is visible to other group members. Your coordinates are never shared.
  • Your email address is never visible to any other user.

5. Location Data

When you grant location permission, your device's GPS coordinates are used temporarily in your browser session to find nearby groups. We then store only a rounded, approximate coordinate (accurate to ~1–2 km) — never your exact position.

You can update or remove your location at any time from your profile settings.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore), which is protected by Firebase's built-in security rules. We enforce strict access controls so that:

  • Only you can read or write your full profile document
  • Group data is accessible only to authenticated members
  • All connections are encrypted via HTTPS and Firebase's TLS layer

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all data we hold about you (visible in your profile)
  • Correct: Update any profile information at any time
  • Delete: Permanently delete your account and all associated data from the Settings tab on your profile page
  • Withdraw consent: Remove your location data at any time from profile settings

8. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication and database — Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap): Reverse geocoding your approximate location to a city name — OSM Privacy Policy
  • EmailJS: Sending group join request notifications — EmailJS Privacy Policy

No other third parties receive your data.

9. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to cache your profile locally for faster loading. This data stays on your device and is cleared when you log out. We do not use tracking cookies or analytics cookies.

10. Children's Privacy

ECA-Connect is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

If we make significant changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of ECA-Connect after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to exercise your data rights, please open an issue on our GitHub repository or contact the project maintainer directly.
